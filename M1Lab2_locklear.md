# The Sandwich Lab

## Items needed to prepare sandwhich
- Peanut Butter
- Knife
- 2 Slices of bread

## Assumptions 
- The 2 Slices of bread are out on a paper towel on the table
- The peanut butter is out on the table
- The knife is out on the table
- The robots hands are empty

## Steps to make a Peanut Butter Sandwhich 
- Pick up the peanut butter and unscrew the lid.
- Place the lid on the table 
- Pick up the knife with the empty hand 
- Scoop a spoonfull of peanut butter
- Place the peanut butter jar down on the table
- Pick up one piece of bread with the empty hand 
- Spread the peanut butter on the slice of bread in your hand
- Spread until even on piece of bread
- Place knife down on the table
- Pick up piece of bread off the table and put it on top of bread with peanut butter

## Done When
- Place sandwhich down on table
